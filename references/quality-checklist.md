# Quality checklist

## Content fidelity

- [ ] Source material was read completely before drafting.
- [ ] Names, metrics, terminology, dates, units, and scope match the source.
- [ ] No unsupported customer claim, outcome, target, or technical capability was added.
- [ ] Conclusions precede supporting detail.
- [ ] Missing information is labeled instead of filled with invented content.
- [ ] Formal Chinese is direct, specific, and free of generic AI marketing phrases.
- [ ] Customer-visible headings state a customer conclusion, construction object, business value, or action direction; none describe how the author should express scope, use evidence, or control commitments.
- [ ] Navigation, eyebrows, labels, captions, statuses, and closing actions contain no unexplained English structural labels or internal consulting jargon.
- [ ] Scope and evidence limitations remain accurate but are written as natural project language in notes, status labels, or boundary modules instead of defensive headlines.

## Narrative and layout

- [ ] The chosen page pattern matches the reader's task.
- [ ] The opening archetype and primary section recipe match the reader's task; unrelated task families do not share one fixed homepage composition.
- [ ] The page has one primary narrative rather than a mixture of templates.
- [ ] A low-copy section-to-form skeleton was reviewed before final wording; relationship, reading axis, object weight, connectors, responsive order, and surface rhythm were decided first.
- [ ] Major modules remain stable with representative short and long Chinese titles, different paragraph lengths, and realistic item counts.
- [ ] Titles, descriptions, evidence, labels, and statuses can be replaced without rebuilding the DOM relationship or rewriting component CSS.
- [ ] Each source block was routed by information shape rather than forced into the nearest visual component.
- [ ] Chapter numbering has a cross-reference or navigation purpose, or is omitted. When used, indexes form one continuous sequence from `01`.
- [ ] Each formal chapter number and its Chinese section label sit together on one line; the number is not stranded in a separate visual column.
- [ ] Chapter numbers and inner phase, step, priority, or milestone numbers remain separate and do not compete.
- [ ] Spatial variation or repetition has a content reason; comparable evidence can share a layout.
- [ ] A section-to-form inventory records the relationship and selection reason; no layout-count quota drove unsupported modules.
- [ ] Ordered phases, milestones, rollout batches, and gates use a timeline, milestone rail, or roadmap rather than a table.
- [ ] Every major block was routed through the thirteen-family `composition-variation-matrix.md`, with the chosen variant justified by relationship, weight, order, condition, responsibility, measurement task, dependency, or feedback direction.
- [ ] Process was not mistaken for timeline; hierarchy was not mistaken for dependency; boundary was not mistaken for status; branching was not rendered as ordinary parallel cards.
- [ ] Repeated variants preserve meaningful comparison; changed information relationships use a suitable form.
- [ ] The template keeps its unique `data-composition-profile`; a related page does not differ only by title, color, or customer name.
- [ ] Tables or matrices serve stable-field comparison or audit tasks; their number follows the evidence, not a quota.
- [ ] Auto-generated contents and reading progress appear only on genuinely long pages and point to stable section IDs.
- [ ] The visible composition distinguishes the hero thesis, section purpose, object groups, evidence or diagrams, metadata, and closing action before close reading.
- [ ] Chinese hero and section titles use deliberate semantic line breaks and leave no one- or two-character orphan line.
- [ ] All visible structural labels in a Chinese deliverable are Chinese unless they are official names, source terms, code identifiers, or requested bilingual text.
- [ ] A proposal or solution hero contains one source-derived project-specific visual when the material supports a loop, hub, boundary, path, or layer relationship.
- [ ] Real topic chips, section counts, and section indexes match the authored taxonomy, quantity, and sequence; none are decorative.
- [ ] No section repeats the same meaning across eyebrow, section label, title, and count.
- [ ] Context panels, summary strips, milestone rails, annotations, code tours, risk ledgers, mockups, and handoff bars are present only when their usage gates in `composition-recipes.md` are satisfied.
- [ ] Structured cards are used for independent, parallel, comparable, or actionable objects and visibly separate visual explanation, title, summary, and evidence, status, ownership, source, or action metadata.
- [ ] Every media block was routed from image count, orientation, relative importance, and shared versus independent captions.
- [ ] Equal landscape evidence, independent portrait items, text-led stories, and unequal-priority images use triptych, portrait cards, compact text-image rows, and lead mosaics respectively when supported.
- [ ] The same media composition is not repeated on the page unless the blocks form a deliberate comparison series.
- [ ] Continuous narrative and relational content use open reading surfaces, rails, diagrams, tables, bands, or other fitting modules instead of being forced into undifferentiated cards.
- [ ] Every diagram, table, or chart materially improves understanding.
- [ ] Empty or unsupported modules from the starting template were removed.
- [ ] If a workbench was used, its task is discovery, comparison, confirmation, explanation, or plan tuning rather than generic decoration.
- [ ] Confirmed workbench choices are written back to a maintained source or handoff instead of existing only in browser state.

## Theme and typography

- [ ] Exactly one brand theme is active.
- [ ] The primary accent carries emphasis; the secondary color appears only in sparse auxiliary marks.
- [ ] Generated imagery and diagrams use the active theme palette; customer brand colors do not leak into a vendor-themed page unless a dual-brand composition was explicitly requested.
- [ ] A Richinfo bilingual text wordmark presents `彩讯科技` in theme orange and `RICHINFO` in neutral charcoal on one baseline.
- [ ] A hero bitmap uses a container proportion suited to its content and does not appear as an unframed opaque-white rectangle pasted onto a tinted first screen.
- [ ] Surface changes identify real groups or transitions; continuous white reading areas are valid and backgrounds are not rotated by count.
- [ ] At most one compact deep theme section carries a sourced thesis, value summary, or transition; it is not a generic dark call to action.
- [ ] No blue-purple gradient, glassmorphism, neon glow, heavy shadow, or multicolor KPI set appears.
- [ ] Every customer-visible authored font-size, including clamp() bounds, is between 16px and 60px inclusive.
- [ ] Body text, captions, notes, navigation, badges, diagram labels, table metadata, and print styles remain at least 16px.
- [ ] Headings, body text, labels, tables, and pull quotes use the same configured Chinese sans-serif stack unless a contrasting typeface was explicitly requested.

## Diagrams and data

- [ ] Architecture layers, callers or channels, horizontal governance, infrastructure boundary, connectors, and flow direction are clear; omit unsupported layers instead of leaving decorative boxes.
- [ ] A signature diagram, when used, explains one real source-derived mechanism and does not duplicate the detailed architecture.
- [ ] Every evidence-chain row includes a named source and date or version, source-faithful fact, effect, bounded response, and any material limitation.
- [ ] Confirmed, candidate, conditional, to-confirm, and out-of-scope labels match the evidence and are not conveyed by color alone.
- [ ] Launch metadata and tier cards appear only when the source supports an actual release and real comparable editions, roles, or paths.
- [ ] Diagram labels remain readable without zooming.
- [ ] KPI definitions, units, periods, and sources are present when available.
- [ ] Tables use restrained rules and preserve critical columns on mobile.
- [ ] Finished charts expose values, definition, period, unit, source, and a text alternative; CSS bars use a zero baseline.
- [ ] Finished tables use captions and scoped header cells; sticky headers do not obscure keyboard focus.
- [ ] Real images have useful `alt`, adjacent captions where needed, and portable paths or embedded data.
- [ ] Media hierarchy remains clear on mobile: shared stories keep one heading, independent cards stay independent, and text-led rows preserve text-first reading order.
- [ ] Color is not the sole carrier of meaning.

## Technical verification

- [ ] HTML opens directly without a build step unless a build was explicitly requested.
- [ ] Relative asset paths resolve correctly.
- [ ] HTML, CSS, and inline SVG have no obvious syntax errors.
- [ ] Desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts were checked.
- [ ] Asymmetric splits, evidence rails, comparisons, roadmaps, process chains, decision trees, responsibility swimlanes, dependency maps, hierarchy branches, cycle loops, media mosaics, and decision ledgers collapse without changing reading order, conditions, ownership, dependency, or feedback direction.
- [ ] Launch tiers, signature diagrams, evidence chains, and maturity registers collapse without hiding status, source, conditions, or connector meaning.
- [ ] No text overflow, overlap, clipped focus ring, or accidental horizontal page scroll remains.
- [ ] Finished delivery contains no unresolved `请替换`, `待填写`, `TBD`, or similar structural placeholders.
- [ ] A claimed single-file delivery contains no unresolved local CSS, JavaScript, image, video, or audio dependency.
- [ ] Skip link, visible keyboard focus, semantic headings, and accessible labels are present.
- [ ] prefers-reduced-motion removes nonessential motion.
- [ ] Print output removes sticky or decorative elements and preserves evidence blocks where practical.
- [ ] Workbench controls have visible text state, keyboard focus, live confirmation, and an understandable non-JavaScript reading fallback.

## Maintenance

- [ ] Reusable updates were made to the smallest appropriate existing file.
- [ ] Any example-derived pattern was stripped of customer names, confidential data, and project metrics.
- [ ] External example sets were audited before absorption and de-duplicated into page archetypes, section recipes, and atomic components instead of copied as fixed templates.
- [ ] The skill validator passes after the change.
- [ ] Automated scans find no forbidden font sizes or unresolved internal references.
- [ ] `scripts/validate_delivery.py` passes in bundle or `--single-file` mode as appropriate.
- [ ] Editor chrome remains the intentional 12px-15px exception; customer-visible authored page content remains within 16px-60px, and clean exports omit the editor.

## 设计复核证据

按 [design-review](design-review.md) 将设计计划与实际画面对照：先检查整体焦点和层次，再检查阅读尺寸、中文断行、装饰的信息作用与移动顺序。记录执行的文件、尺寸、问题和修正；未实际完成的视觉或交互项目标注为未验证。
